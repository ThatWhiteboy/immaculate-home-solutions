Terms of Service

How booking works

  • Referral-only. Immaculate Home Solutions takes new homes only by referral from a current client. There is no public booking and no sign-up for strangers.
  • Angela approves every booking, personally. Sending a request is not a booking. Nothing is scheduled or confirmed until Angela reviews the request and confirms it herself.
  • Scope of service. Angela provides regular and deep cleaning for homes on her route. She does not offer move-in or move-out cleaning herself — if you need that, she’s happy to refer you to someone she trusts who does that work.
  • Availability is Angela’s call. She may pause new requests, keep a waitlist, or decline a request that isn’t a fit, at any time.
  • Clear identification. Every message clearly comes from Angela / Immaculate Home Solutions — from her own phone number and the email on her business card (immaculatehome@myyahoo.com) — never from an unfamiliar number or sender.
  • Consent before messaging. Confirmations and reminders are only sent to people who have agreed to be contacted (for example, by giving Angela their number and asking for a clean, or agreeing to reminders as a client). Agreeing to messages is never a condition of getting your home cleaned — you can always reach Angela directly instead.

Privacy Policy

Your information stays yours

  • What’s collected: your name, phone number, and email if you give one; the name of the client who referred you; and the home details you share in a request (home size, pets, entry notes, and what you’d like cleaned).
  • What it’s used for: only to review your request, schedule and carry out cleans, and send the confirmations and reminders you agreed to.
  • Never sold. Your contact information is never sold, rented, or shared for anyone else’s marketing. Ever.
  • Referrals only with your say-so: if you ask about a move-in or move-out clean, Angela passes your contact details to the person she refers you to only if you tell her to.
  • Your call on your info: ask Angela anytime what she has on file, or ask her to delete it, at immaculatehome@myyahoo.com.

Message preferences — opt out anytime, instantly

You can stop messages from Immaculate Home Solutions at any time, for any reason, and it takes effect immediately — no questions, no waiting period, and it never affects your cleaning service.

  • Texts: reply STOP to any text (END, CANCEL, QUIT, or UNSUBSCRIBE work too). You’ll get one short confirmation and no further texts.
  • Email: use the opt-out / unsubscribe link or button at the bottom of any email — one click, honored immediately.
  • Or just tell Angela — in person, by phone, or by email — and she’ll take you off the list herself, right away.
